What this test cannot establish

Historical data only. SMART encodings, hardware, and fleet composition change. No current-fleet external validation was performed.
Validation prevalence is 0.104%; test prevalence is 0.499%. The source population drops sharply from 2013-08-20 through 2013-10-14, for an unknown reason. This is a meaningful transfer risk even within one archive.
Bootstrap intervals resample drives and describe test-period drive-sampling variation; they do not capture time dependence or source shift. Daily alerts have not been collapsed into operational episodes.
